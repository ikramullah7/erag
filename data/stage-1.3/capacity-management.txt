# Payments capacity management RB-CAP-045

Owner: Platform Capacity | Dataset: stage-1.3-v1 | Status: synthetic training fixture

## Production planning budget

The monthly planning budget for the payments production platform is 12000 USD. The budget alert fires when forecast spend exceeds 80 percent of that budget. A budget alert does not cap spending or stop workloads. Platform Capacity owns capacity forecasts; Finance approves budget increases. This policy records a planning budget, not actual current spend.

## Node scale boundaries

Review available subnet addresses and workload disruption budgets before requesting more node capacity. The production paynp autoscaler policy remains in the AKS runbook; this planning policy does not authorize bypassing its limits. Increasing a node pool maximum does not fix an untolerated scheduling taint or an application memory leak.

## Archived planning assumption

An archived sandbox forecast used a monthly budget of 2000 USD. That sandbox assumption does not apply to production. No current invoice total, live forecast reading or actual number of free subnet addresses is documented here.
